About the Photo

Ronaldo is in Portugal's red home shirt during a night game against Argentina, arms out to balance himself as the ball drops in front of his chest. The stadium behind him is thrown out of focus, so your eye goes straight to him. It is 900 by 600 pixels and 97 KB.

About the JPG Format

JPG (or JPEG) is the format cameras and phones save to, built for photographs.

  • Colors: about 16.7 million
  • Compression: lossy — it permanently discards detail to shrink the file
  • Transparency: not supported
  • Animation: not supported

It suits this image because the blurred crowd and grass are thousands of slightly different greens and reds, and losing a little detail inside a blur is invisible. Nothing here needs to be see-through either, so the lack of transparency costs me nothing.

Why I Chose Him

Ronaldo was the first player I ever looked up to, and he is the reason I started playing. It was never just the goals — it was the mentality, the discipline, and the fact that he showed up in the biggest moments every time. As a kid I would watch him and then go outside and try to copy his moves and his celebration. He is my GOAT.
