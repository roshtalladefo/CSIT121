What Is Happening in the Photo

Mbappé is in France's dark blue home kit with the number 10 on the front, caught mid-game as he turns his head to look at something off to his right. It is a daytime match and the stands behind him are completely full, so the background is a wall of out-of-focus color. You can still pick out the small details on the shirt, like the tournament badge on his chest and the French flag on the sleeve. The photo is 900 by 600 pixels and the file is about 867 KB.

About the PNG Format

PNG stands for Portable Network Graphics. It was created as a free replacement for the older GIF format and it is the format people usually reach for when quality matters more than file size.

Key Traits

  • Colors: up to about 16.7 million, the same range as JPG
  • Compression: lossless, so nothing is thrown away and the image is rebuilt pixel for pixel
  • Transparency: fully supported, including partly see-through edges
  • Animation: not supported in normal PNG files

Why PNG Suits This Image

This shot has a lot of small, sharp details on the shirt that I did not want softened, and because PNG is lossless every one of them survives exactly as the camera recorded it. It also means I could re-save this file as many times as I wanted without it slowly getting worse, which is not true of JPG.

The honest trade-off is size. At 867 KB this page is by far the heaviest of the three, because lossless compression cannot do much with a busy photo of a crowd. For a real website I would probably use JPG or WebP here instead, and save PNG for logos and graphics that need transparency.

Why I Chose This Image

Mbappé is the player I enjoy watching most right now because of how fast he is, and I wanted a picture that showed him thinking instead of sprinting. This one caught my eye because he is clearly reading the game and looking for the next pass. The blue France kit against the blurry crowd also just looks really clean to me.
