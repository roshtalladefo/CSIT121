About the Image

Mbappé is in France's dark blue number 10 shirt, turning his head to look at something off to his right. The stands behind him are packed, so the background is a wall of out-of-focus color. It is 900 by 600 pixels and 867 KB.

Image file type information
Format PNG (Portable Network Graphics)
Colors about 16.7 million, the same range as JPG
Compression lossless — nothing is thrown away
Transparency fully supported, including soft edges
Animation not supported

Being lossless keeps the small details on the shirt exactly as the camera recorded them. The trade-off is size: at 867 KB this is the heaviest image on the site, so on a real site I would use JPG or WebP here and save PNG for graphics that need transparency.

Why I Chose Him

Mbappé feels like the modern version of Ronaldo to me — young, fearless, and completely ready for the big stage. He performs in the moments that actually matter, and when adversity comes he seems to welcome it instead of hiding from it. He is also just fast, explosive and dangerous every time he touches the ball.
